# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and horror series created by the Duffer Brothers.  
It’s set in the 1980s and follows a group of kids who uncover strange experiments and supernatural events in their small town.

### Main Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair

### Why I Like It
- The story is exciting and mysterious.
- I enjoy the 80s setting and music.
- The friendships between the characters are inspiring.

> “Friends don’t lie.” – Eleven
